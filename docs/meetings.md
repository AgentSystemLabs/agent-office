# Meetings

## Continuing a stopped meeting

A stopped meeting does not advance just because its terminals are ready again. Open Meeting room and choose **Continue meeting**. All original participants must still be at the table and ready; if someone is asleep, open/resume that terminal first. The original meeting owner can continue a meeting owned by an account. No replacement workers or worktrees are created. Existing round contributions are retained; only missing parts are handed out again, with the meeting context restored for sessions that have no history. Missing participants require a new meeting; saved notes remain available.

The 3D speech bubbles show **MEETING STOPPED** or **MEETING COMPLETE** independently of the terminal. Their text also states whether a terminal is still active, asleep, waiting for input or ready. An active terminal does not imply the stopped meeting has resumed. Continuing is explicit, never automatic after a server restart.
